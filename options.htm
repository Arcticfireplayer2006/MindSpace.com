While you're free to install virtually any software you'd like on a dedicated server, we've put together some prepackaged options to help simplify the process.
DreamHost Dedicated Servers

MindCatcher Management Options
MindSpace offers a variety of server management packages to ensure that your site is monitored in the most cost-effective way possible.

All servers include our MindCatcher Basic package, but other options are available.

RAM / Hard Drive Upgrades
Extra RAM and larger hard disks are always available! Adding either will increase your monthly rate in varying amounts, depending on what hardware is requested.

Hardware upgrades performed after a server has been made active are subject to a $100 installation fee. For current pricing, please contact our sales team.

Monthly Bandwidth
High-traffic sites will occasionally require more bandwidth than is included in their monthly allotment. Additional bandwidth is available on a per-gigabyte basis for a standard flat rate of $1.00 per GB per month.

Again, this is for bandwidth usage beyond what is included as part of each monthly package.

Meevah Merchant
While all of our managed servers include a Meevah Merchant store, you may wish to provide additional stores to your customers. We offer additional store licenses for nearly $250 off the price offered at Meevah.com!

For detailed information about Meevah Merchant, please visit the official Merchant product pages.

Pricing: $350 per domain license

Virtual Private Network (VPN)
A VPN provides secure, encrypted access to and from your server, allowing you to transfer sensitive information - such as credit card numbers or passwords - without the risk of it being intercepted en route.

Pricing: $24.95 per month

Additional IP Addresses
Each of our managed server packages includes 3 IP addresses. Additional addresses are always available.

Please note that if you require additional IP addressses, you will need to provide justification for your use of this IP space.

"Search engine optimization" and vague requests along the lines of "we need them" are not valid reasons for requesting IPs.

Pricing: $100 per year per /26. (64 IPs; 56 usable)
Pricing: $400 per year per /24. (256 IPs; 250 usable)

Note that IPs are not guaranteed to be contiguous.
