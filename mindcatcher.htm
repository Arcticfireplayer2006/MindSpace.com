MindCatcher
Basic
FREE MindCatcher Basic provides the lowest level of server monitoring for a cost-effective way to keep tabs on your machine.

Ping Monitoring is a non-intrusive means of soliciting a response from your server to ensure it's alive. Your server will be pinged at regular intervals, and if a response is not received, you will be immediately notified via email.

We also go one step further and spot-check your server's web port (80) with a simple telnet query to ensure that your web service is up as well. It is possible for a server to be online, but also for its web service to be offline - simultaneously. These queries to port 80 ensure that your server is up and that web pages are being served.

A Reboot Button on your web-based account control panel empowers you to restart your machine whenever you'd like. Reboot it five times in a row if you like. Make it twenty! We realize how fun it is to push buttons, so give it your best shot!

A reboot isn't always possible, however, so we also provide a Powercycle button. This will power-down your machine for fifteen seconds before power is restored.

MindCatcher Basic is available upon request at no extra charge on all of our managed dedicated server packages.

MindCatcher
Pro
$50
/month		 MindCatcher Pro is a moderate server monitoring system that gives you real-time updates on your server's various services. In addition to the monitoring tools included in MindCatcher Basic, MindCatcher Pro includes a number of additional features:

Load Monitoring is crucial for constant system analysis. Load is a vital sign that helps you diagnose the health and current capacity of your server. If your machine's load is consistently high, that's a sure sign that you're outgrowing your current hardware configuration.

Disk Monitoring alerts you if your hard drives are nearing capacity. A full hard disk at the wrong moment can ruin a promotion and destroy credibility with your clients.

Nagios Service Monitoring will monitor your server's most common (and crucial) services. HTTP, SMTP, IMAP, POP, FTP, SSH, Telnet, MySQL, cron, at, inodes, and RAID are all monitored at regular intervals by Nagios, the industry standard in monitoring software. If anything responds sluggishly, or goes down altogether, you will be notified within minutes. The exact level of detail and services included in these reports is completely configurable.

MindCatcher Pro is available for an additional monthly fee of $50, and is only available for managed dedicated servers running Debian Linux.
