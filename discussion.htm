Welcome to the MindSpace Discussion Forum!

Guidelines:

Please understand that these forums are not routinely checked by MindSpace employees (although we do show up from time to time!), and are not meant as a replacement for the Hippocampus or support form. While a great way to receive help and insight from other MindSpace customers, you should use the above options if you need a fast or official response.

Do not post information such as passwords or billing information on these forums. Obviously, that kind of stuff is generally best kept private.

Users cannot post until they create an account, so you should register as soon as possible. Forum accounts are separate from your MindSpace NDN ID or user login accounts.

All posts should be kept on topic as much as possible. While the occasional tangent is perfectly fine, please try to stay within the realm of the forum you post to. A special catch-all forum called "Offtopic Discussion" has been created specifically for off-topic posting.

MindSpace is used by customers of all ages and backgrounds, so we request that you keep the language that you use family-friendly.

The information posted on this forum is not guaranteed to be reliable, and you should be careful whenever using information provided by third parties. Always keep local backups of your site content.

Spamming or illegal activity will not be tolerated, and violators will be reported.

Last but not least: Be nice!
